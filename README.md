# Album 71 - Veils of Eternity: Songs of Light &amp; Deliverance

A diverse worship and spiritual soundtrack blending simple children’s praise, multilingual Christian anthems, deliverance and revival imagery, contemporary cultural commentary reclaimed with hope, and prophetic songs that confront darkness with the light of Christ. Musical styles and languages vary across the record—children’s praise, Slavic and Central/Eastern European devotional poetry, high-energy spirit-filled worship, prophetic deliverance, and imaginative narrative songs—unified by themes of God’s love, the Holy Spirit, redemption, and the triumph of light over darkness.

## Tracks

Track 1: Jesus Loves Us — A joyful children’s praise hymn celebrating Jesus’ love and comfort.

Track 2: Matrix of the Ancients — Multilingual prophetic hymn breaking pagan strongholds and declaring the Trinity’s light.

Track 3: Holy Spirit — Light Divine — Prophetic worship anthem of angelic deliverance and cosmic renewal.

Track 4: In the Feeds — Spirit Over the Noise — Contemporary cultural critique transformed into a call for Spirit-led unity and freedom.

Track 5: Tartar Light — Steppe-inspired deliverance hymn dismantling idolatry and restoring the land.

Track 6: Galaxy of Hope — Imaginative, cinematic praise celebrating redemption and the hero’s journey in cosmic metaphor.

Track 7: Veil of Eternity — A solemn, worldwide plea for Christ’s light to unveil and redeem.
